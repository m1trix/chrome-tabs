Domain rules

“Group by domain” (popup button or Alt+Shift+G) puts tabs matching a rule into the named group with its color. Subdomains match too: github.com also matches gist.github.com. Rules can share a group name to combine several domains.

Browsers only allow nine tab group colors. Custom colors appear in this extension and are shown as the closest built-in color in the tab strip.
